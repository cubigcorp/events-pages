CUBIG was selected as one of six startups in the 2025 AI Collabo Open Ground. The Jeju Center for Creative Economy and Innovation hosts the program, which was designed with DB Insurance, Chokwang Paint and Hana Bank.

Selected startups work with a matched corporate partner through December on technical tests. CUBIG was matched with DB Insurance.

What CUBIG brought

  1. 01LLM Capsule work with DB Insurance for generative AI use in insurance work
  2. 02LLM Capsule finds sensitive values, replaces them with context-preserving substitutes, and restores original values in the response
  3. 03Scope to explore together: secure document processing, summarization, and insurance-specific AI workflows
