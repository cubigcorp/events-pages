CUBIG won Best of Demo Day at the 2024 AI Security Day. The Ministry of Science and ICT and KISA (Korea Internet & Security Agency) hosted the event.

CUBIG demoed two products at the event: DTS and Azoo. DTS prepares data locally with drag-and-drop steps, without exposing the source data. Azoo is a web marketplace where companies trade, analyze and evaluate datasets.
