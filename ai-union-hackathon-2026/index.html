CUBIG joined the 2026 AI Univ. Union Hackathon at Korea University in Seoul on November 21, 2026, as sponsor and technology partner. CUBIG provided Syntitan to participating teams, gave technical advice, and served on the judging panel.

Students from Korea University, Sogang University, Seoul National University of Science and Technology, and Ewha Womans University formed cross-university teams to build AI services. From the November 1 orientation to the final event, CUBIG helped teams prepare the data their services needed with Syntitan.
