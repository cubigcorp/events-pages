George Firican, founder of Lights On Data, sat down with Ho Bae to talk about why AI that works in a demo struggles once it meets real company data.

The conversation starts from one question: if the data is AI-ready, ready for what? The same dataset can work for one AI job and fail for another. They walk through real examples, from service case follow-up decisions to churn prediction and a food supply quoting project.

What they talked about

  1. 01What AI-ready means, and why it depends on the task
  2. 02Filling missing cases with DTS, and why naive synthetic data fails
  3. 03Swapping sensitive values for context-preserving stand-ins with LLM Capsule before they reach AI
  4. 04Checking whether prepared data actually helps the task, and approval criteria over metrics
  5. 05Build vs. buy, and reusing what still applies
