Ho Bae joined Ravit Jain on The Ravit Show to talk about what makes enterprise data AI-ready.

Most companies already have plenty of data. What they lack is data an AI task can actually use. Ho also walked through a live demo, tracing two network alerts back to the same device without the model ever seeing the original device name.

What they talked about

  1. 01Why having data and having AI-ready data are two different things
  2. 02Why AI-ready depends on the task, not data in general
  3. 03Why removing too much data can break the AI by stripping out relationships
  4. 04How LLM Capsule returns results inside the workflow with the real business values
  5. 05Keeping your current model, and starting with one blocked AI task
