CUBIG took part in Singapore FinTech Festival from November 11 to 13, 2025, with Seoul Fintech Lab support, in the Invest Seoul pavilion.

On the first day, the program held a demo day and meetup with 15 Singapore VCs. At the booth, fintech teams asked mostly about DTS and Syntitan.
