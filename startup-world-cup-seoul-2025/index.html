CUBIG won the Seoul regional final of Startup World Cup 2025 and advanced to the Grand Finale in Silicon Valley, October 15–17, 2025.

Startup World Cup, run by Pegasus Tech Ventures, is one of the largest startup pitch competitions in the world, with a $1 million grand prize.

Ho Bae, CUBIG co-founder and CEO, said the team saw the Startup World Cup as the fastest path into the U.S. market.

What CUBIG brought

  1. 01DTS and LLM Capsule as CUBIG’s core products
  2. 02A plan to use the Grand Finale in Silicon Valley as the first step into the U.S. market
